# Governance

Wayloop is maintained by its founding maintainer, who has final say on scope and design while
the project is young. As the community grows, maintainers are added for areas (`core`,
`runtime`, `tracker`, `sdk`, `docs`) based on sustained, high-quality contributions.

- Design decisions are discussed publicly in issues and recorded as ADRs in
  `docs/en/project/adr/`.
- Changes to the engine contracts (schemas of artifacts, events and commands) require approval
  from a maintainer of `crates/wayloop-core`.
- Contact: open a discussion or use the maintainers' contact on the GitHub profile.
