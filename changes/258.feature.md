`wayloop validate` now checks gate issues, milestones, epics, user stories and tasks against
their published schemas, as it already did for briefs. Each refusal names the faulty field and
why: a gate marked done without the person who approved it or its issue number, a story approved
without its specification, a milestone, epic, story or task under way without its tracker
number, a task verified by a judge without a rubric, a task without its epic, or an identifier
in the wrong form, with the corrected value when one can be derived. Prose may be written in any
language; keys, statuses and other fixed values stay in English, and a translated key such as
`titolo` is refused with the English key as the fix. Whether linked artifacts exist and whether
an area belongs to the project policy are not checked yet: the result lists these checks as not
done.
