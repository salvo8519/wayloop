`wayloop validate` now checks pull requests and commit messages sent as inputs against their
published schemas, `schemas/inputs/pull_request/v1.json` and `schemas/inputs/commit/v1.json`.
The `input_kind` of each entry chooses the schema. Every input carries its `schema_version`, as
an artifact does, and is checked against the schema of that version. A pull request needs a
title, a body (which may be empty), a branch and a list of labels (which may be empty); a commit
needs a header and may have a body. The title, the branch, each label, the header and a body
of a commit that is given cannot be empty. A missing, empty or unknown field is refused with its name. The
form of branch names and commit headers, and the issue linked by a pull request, are not checked
yet: the result lists these checks as not done.
