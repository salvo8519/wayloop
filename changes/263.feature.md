`wayloop validate` now checks the links between artifacts. Every link resolves by identifier,
whatever the file or folder of its target: a link to an identifier no artifact of the set
carries is refused with `link_missing`, and a link to a superseded artifact with
`link_superseded`, which lists its replacements in `expected` and, when there is exactly one,
gives it as the fix. The requirements a task or a change delta cites, and those an acceptance
criterion of a living specification names, must be requirements of that specification in
force. A task or a story whose milestone differs from that of its epic, or a task whose epic
differs from that of its story, is refused with `hierarchy_mismatch`. An artifact that is done
or superseded, and a hand-over record, is not changed again: its broken links are the warning
`link_broken_terminal`. A document with an error of its own is not checked for links, so that
one broken file does not cause a wave of errors. When the numbers of the ADRs of the project
are given in `adrs`, a citation such as `ADR 0002, 0003` in `related` of a living specification
that names no ADR gives the new warning `adr_citation_missing`. The check `links` moves from
`diagnostics.not_done` to `diagnostics.checks`; the check `adr_citations` runs when `adrs` is
given and is listed as not done otherwise.
