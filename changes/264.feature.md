`wayloop validate` now checks the parts of artifacts and the operations of change deltas, with
the check `parts`. A part identifier given twice in its list (a decision or a question of a
council report, a requirement, an acceptance criterion or an open question), a dissent that
names a decision the report does not have, and a recommendation or an answer outside the
options of its question are refused with `part_invalid`. A task that depends on itself, on a
superseded task (with its replacement as the fix when there is one) or on a task that depends
on it again is refused too; a task that is done or superseded is not checked. A change delta
with two operations on one requirement is refused with `delta_conflict`, and an operation that
carries a member its `op` refuses, such as `text` on `removed`, with `artifact_schema`. A task
verified by `none` beside a technique is refused. The engine also gives the identifier of the
next requirement of a living specification, after the highest ever used, removed ones
included, for the merge of change deltas. The check `parts` moves from `diagnostics.not_done`
to `diagnostics.checks`.
