`wayloop validate` now checks the titles of epics and tasks. A title with a type prefix of the
policy (`feat(core):`), a bracket tag at its start or end (`[EPIC]`) or a final full stop is
refused with `title_format`, one error per rule, with the corrected title in `fix`. Two epic or
task titles that are equal after Unicode normalisation, case folding and white space are both
refused with `title_duplicate`, each naming the other; superseded epics and tasks are not
compared. A title that starts with a command name, such as `wayloop init`, passes. When the
input gives no policy and the set holds an epic or a task, the default policy, the policy of
the Wayloop repository, applies and is listed in `meta.defaults_applied` with the reason
`default_policy`.
