# ADR 0016: Expert council: independent first round and structured report

Status: Proposed · 2026-11-17 (milestone M4 · Upstream planning; decision tracked in #92, #98)

## Context

A council generated by one context anchors on its first opinion and hides conflicts.

## Decision

Roles answer the brief independently in a fixed format, then read only summaries of the others, then a moderator writes a report with decisions, rejected alternatives, dissent, risks and questions. Unresolved conflicts go to the maintainer.

## Consequences

Conflicts reach the person who decides. Cost is bounded by format and length.
