# Brand

## The mark

A **loop of work that passes through a gate**, on a navy seal. The white ring is the loop; the
amber diamond sitting in its opening is the human gate, where the loop stops for a decision;
the teal token is the current step moving along the loop. The mark tells the product in one
picture: work moves on its own, and people decide at the gates.

The gate is a diamond, not a bar: a bar across the opening of a ring reads as a power symbol.
The mark stays legible at 16 pixels, so it is also the favicon.

Files live in `docs/_shared/assets/brand/` and are generated by `scripts/brand.py`:

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `mark.svg` | Primary mark, on light and dark backgrounds; favicon |
| `mark-inverted.svg` | Mark on navy surfaces where a navy seal would disappear |
| `logo-light.svg` / `logo-dark.svg` | Horizontal lockup (mark and wordmark) |
| `banner-light.svg` / `banner-dark.svg` | README and announcements |
| `social-preview.svg` / `social-preview.png` | Repository social preview (1280 by 640) |

Keep clear space around the mark of at least a quarter of its width. Do not recolour, stretch,
rotate or add effects. Text in the SVG files is converted to outlines: no font is needed.

## Name and voice

- **Name:** Wayloop. The way is the roadmap, travelled stage by stage; the loop is the work
  that runs until each stage is verified.
- **Category:** Spec-driven agentic loop.
- **Promise:** From idea to merged code, in your issues, with humans at the gates.
- **Voice:** corporate and precise. Short sentences, explicit terms, no superlatives, no
  exclamation marks, no icons or emoji.

## Palette

| Name | Hex | Role | Contrast |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ink Navy | `#0B1F3A` | Primary: the seal, headings, dark surfaces | 16.5:1 on white |
| Deep Navy | `#12325C` | Interactive states on light surfaces | 12.8:1 on white |
| Route Teal | `#0F766E` | Links and accents on light surfaces | 5.5:1 on white |
| Loop Teal | `#2DD4BF` | The token in the mark; accent on dark surfaces | 9.7:1 on Night |
| Gate Amber | `#F59E0B` | Gates and approval states, on navy only | 7.7:1 on Ink Navy |
| Slate | `#475569` | Secondary text | 7.6:1 on white |
| Night | `#0A1628` | Dark backgrounds | |
| Paper | `#F7F8FA` | Light backgrounds | |

Gate Amber is never used for text on white: it reaches 2.1:1 and fails WCAG 2.1 AA. Every text
colour above meets AA (4.5:1) on the background it is meant for.

## Typography

- **Inter** (700 for the wordmark, 600 headings, 400 body): interface and documents.
- **JetBrains Mono** (500): code, identifiers, commands.

Both are open source under the SIL Open Font License.

## Badges

README badges use the palette: Ink Navy for facts about the product, Deep Navy for the
technology, Route Teal for the way of working, Gate Amber for the licence.
